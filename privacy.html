Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Glimpse is a community opinion poll site. This page explains what we collect and why. Contact: aliyufaruk483@gmail.com.

What we collect

Votes and comments you post, including the handle you choose. Handles are public. Do not put your real name or contact details in comments.

A random anonymous ID created in your browser, which lets us count one vote per person per poll and apply comment limits. It is not linked to your name, phone number or email.

When you vote, we ask which Nigerian state you are voting from and store it with your anonymous vote, so we can see anonymous results by region. It is your choice: you can pick "Prefer not to say" or change it at any time. We do not use GPS or look up your location from your IP address for this. If you choose a state, it may also appear as a tag next to your comments, and votes are shown publicly only as totals per state.

Data stored on your device (local storage) to remember your votes and handle.

Usage statistics through Google Analytics, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location.

Advertising

We may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Where data is stored

Votes and comments are stored with Supabase, our database provider. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

Comments and votes are kept while the site runs. You can ask us to delete a comment by emailing us with the handle and the text.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you may ask what data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us to do so.

Children

Glimpse is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
